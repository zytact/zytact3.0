# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

This portfolio serves all credibility-seeking audiences: recruiters, hiring managers, founders, clients, and developer peers. Visitors are usually scanning quickly, looking for evidence that Arnab can take ambiguous ideas from zero to shipped software across frontend, backend, AI integrations, and tooling.

## Product Purpose

The site exists to turn proof of work into trust. It should make Arnab feel like a full-stack builder with visible shipping history, technical range, and enough taste to make the work memorable. Success means visitors quickly understand what he builds, see credible project depth, and know where to inspect work or make contact.

## Brand Personality

Playful, technical, relentless. The voice should feel maker-ish and direct: energetic without becoming chaotic, technical without hiding behind terminal cosplay, and confident without corporate polish.

## Anti-references

Avoid terminal or ANSI aesthetics, generic SaaS landing pages, purple gradients, corporate recruiter templates, overly artsy portfolios that hide the work, and minimal black-and-white clone portfolios.

## Design Principles

1. Show shipped proof before abstract claims.
2. Keep the builder energy visible, but never let decoration obscure project evidence.
3. Make technical range scannable for busy visitors and inspectable for technical peers.
4. Use playful details as memory hooks, not as noise.
5. Prefer direct copy that says what was built, why it matters, and where to verify it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use sane accessibility defaults: semantic structure, keyboard-readable controls, readable contrast, responsive layouts, descriptive link text, alt text when imagery is used, and reduced-motion alternatives for nonessential motion.
